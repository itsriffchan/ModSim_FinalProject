# LGU seal files

Accessed 2026-11-23 from Wikimedia Commons. Each PNG is a local copy of the Commons thumbnail (about 512 px on the long side; Malabon is the original 175×178 file because Commons has no larger render). These marks identify the saved LGU in the region picker. They are not geographic evidence, building data, or a damage forecast.

Commons labels every included file **Public domain**, as a work of the Philippine government or a local government unit under Republic Act No. 8293, section 171.11 and section 176. Section 176 still requires prior approval of the creating office before the work is exploited for profit. Official insignia can also be restricted apart from copyright. This academic viewer does not grant any further right to reuse the seals.
